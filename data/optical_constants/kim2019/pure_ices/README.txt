These are composite optical data (n and k) of pure ices, i.e., amorphous ice and crystalline ice. 

1st, 2nd, and 3rd columns indicate the wavelength, n (responsible for scattering), and k (responsible for emission), respectively. 
Note that crystalline ice data is temperature dependent from 62 microns (Reinert et al. 2015)

You can find more details, e.g., reference of each data, in Kim et al. 2019 "Constrainging the detectibility of water ice in debris disks"
